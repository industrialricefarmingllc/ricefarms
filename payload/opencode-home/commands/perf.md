---
description: Improve performance to a maximum
---

Could you perform a full, thorough, both measured and theoretical, performance analysis of $ARGUMENTS? Predict the actual frequency of computations over a period of time and the individual occurence's processing time. Try to reduce both processing time and frequency to an absolute minimum without degrading effective functionality and never at the cost of responsiveness to the end user. It might help to think about this as if you're optimizing a project euler problem.
